How we work

Co-authorship by default

Every contributing institution receives co-authorship on flagship papers. Cohort PIs sit on the publications committee.

Data stays with you

Federated-first. We ship the training and evaluation container; slides and clinical data never leave your firewall unless you opt in.

IRB & DTA ready

Template DTA, IRB protocol language, and a HIPAA-compliant pipeline on day one. The legal groundwork is done.
